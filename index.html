02 / The problem

DeFi has expanded. The experience remains fragmented.

Digital assets and decentralised finance have opened up new ways to access, invest, and manage capital. But the tools needed to do so are often spread across different networks, protocols, and applications.

Research happens in one place, trading in another, and portfolio management somewhere else. Connecting these activities can be complex and time-consuming.

Olive is designed to make these activities easier to access and manage.

00 / Foundation

The wallet is the foundation.

Olive Wallet is the account, asset-access and transaction-authorization layer that every capability runs through. It is the starting point, not a fifth pillar.

Connected DeFi, Quantitative Investments, Agentic Trading, and Asset and Portfolio Management each do a different job. Together they form one path: access the market, understand the opportunity, execute within your limits, and oversee the result.

  • AccessSupported networks, assets and protocols.
  • IntelligenceResearch, models and investment analysis.
  • ExecutionAuthorized agents acting within limits.
  • OversightPortfolio visibility, exposure and control.

RWA marketplace

Real-world assets are an investment category accessed through the four pillars, not a separate pillar. Olive's intended model is a third-party marketplace: external issuers remain responsible for their products, specialist partners may provide tokenization, custody, administration, verification, payment and settlement, and Olive provides the marketplace, wallet connectivity, research and portfolio interface. Olive does not initially position itself as the originator, issuer, custodian or operator of a proprietary secondary exchange.

Illustrative architecture. Olive is in development.

01 / Access

Connected DeFi

One coherent way into decentralised finance.

Connected DeFi is Olive's access layer to supported blockchain networks, protocols, digital assets and financial opportunities. It is designed to reduce the need to move between disconnected applications and interfaces.

Users can reach supported networks and assets through the wallet, explore token swaps and decentralised trading where liquidity and infrastructure allow, and access DeFi services such as exchanges, staking, lending, borrowing and yield.

  • MultichainSupported networks and assets in one interface.
  • Cross-chainConnect otherwise separate blockchain environments.
  • SwapsTrading access subject to supported liquidity and protocols.
  • ProtocolsDEX, staking, lending, borrowing and yield access.
  • DiscoveryExplore supported tokens, protocols and opportunities.

RWA access

Connected DeFi is also the access point for tokenized real-world asset products available through Olive's intended marketplace. Transferability, trading, liquidity, collateral use, distributions and redemption depend on the product's legal structure, issuer terms and infrastructure.

Explore Connected DeFi

02 / Intelligence

Quantitative Investments

Turn market insight into investment opportunities.

Quantitative Investments is Olive's research, analytical and investment-intelligence layer. It is designed to help users understand markets, evaluate opportunities, assess assets and develop systematic strategies.

Market and on-chain data, quantitative models, statistical analysis and backtesting where reliable historical data exists come together to support strategy design, risk and return analysis, and asset or protocol research.

  • MarketsPrices, performance, trends, history and on-chain data.
  • ResearchModels, statistics, comparisons and measurable opportunities.
  • StrategiesDesign, backtesting, evaluation and refinement.
  • AssetsMarket structure, liquidity and protocol risk.
  • RWAIssuer, underlying, structure, income, valuation, liquidity and risk.

RWA evaluation

For RWA products, the analytical layer helps compare issuers, underlying assets, legal structures, income mechanics, pricing context, liquidity, redemption terms and risk. A token may represent a legal interest, debt claim, beneficial interest, asset-backed claim or synthetic exposure — not necessarily direct ownership. Projected income, historical performance and estimated valuations are not guarantees.

Potential categories — not confirmed at launch: government securities, real estate, commodities, private credit, tokenized funds and eligible equity.

Explore Quantitative Investments

03 / Execution

Agentic Trading

Authorised strategies, carried out within your limits.

Agentic Trading is Olive's intelligent execution layer. Agents are designed to analyse conditions, identify opportunities that fit an assigned strategy, and execute authorised activity within user-defined objectives, limits and permissions.

Users can discover and configure agents, assign a strategy, set risk preferences, capital limits and operating conditions, and monitor activity, positions and performance. Authority stays with the user.

  • DiscoveryPurpose, markets, strategies and operating characteristics.
  • ConfigurationObjectives, assets, strategy, risk, limits and permissions.
  • AssignmentDefine the conditions under which an agent may operate.
  • MonitoringMarket conditions, prices, liquidity and signals.
  • ExecutionPermitted actions within granted limits.

Conditional RWA execution

For supported RWA products, agents may monitor opportunities, track availability and conditions, alert users, compare products and suggest allocations. Execution is conditional on the issuer, marketplace, payment and settlement infrastructure. RWA products do not all trade continuously or settle instantly; an agent must not treat them as if they do.

Explore Agentic Trading

04 / Oversight

Asset and Portfolio Management

Understand your assets. Manage your capital.

Asset and Portfolio Management is Olive's unified financial oversight and capital-management layer. It brings supported digital assets, tokenized RWA investments, positions and financial activity into one view.

The portfolio is designed to explain the position, not just display a dashboard: what is held, how it is changing, what income or events are occurring, and how capital is distributed across supported networks and products.

  • PortfolioDigital assets, tokenized RWA investments, positions and consolidated visibility.
  • CapitalAllocation, exposure, position management, rebalancing and optimisation.
  • PerformanceHistory, P&L where reliable data exists, asset-level and allocation analysis.
  • ActivityTransactions, deposits, withdrawals, distributions and income events.
  • MonitoringAlerts, changes, maturity notifications, redemption status and issuer updates.
  • RWA lifecycleProduct, issuer, rights, valuation and date, fees, maturity, redemption and disclosures.

Product-specific terms

RWA holdings appear alongside other supported assets. For each investment, users can inspect the product and issuer, underlying asset, legal or economic rights, amount, current reported valuation and date, income, fees, maturity, redemption conditions and status, transfer restrictions and relevant disclosures.

Valuation frequency, liquidity, income structures and redemption processes differ by product. No instant liquidation or automatic rebalancing is implied.

Explore Asset & Portfolio Management

Delegate activity. Retain authority.

Intelligent financial tools should expand what users can do without removing their control.

Olive is being built around user-defined objectives, permissions, capital limits, and risk parameters. These controls shape what agents are authorised to do, while users retain oversight of their financial activity.
