CLV migration scams: official links and red flags

Published 2026-11-19 · Updated 2026-11-19 · 722 words

CLV migration scams usually arrive as direct messages, lookalike domains, or fake support offering to speed up a migration. The real test is simple. Only olivefinance.org, wallet.olivefinance.org and t.me/olive_eng are official. No support person asks for a seed phrase, and no official address is sent in a direct message.

A CLV migration scam follows a predictable pattern. A lot of people are looking for an address at the same time, and that is the opportunity an impersonator waits for.

This page is not about naming anyone. It is about the patterns, so you can recognise one before it costs you. Status checked 19 November 2026.

The three common patterns

The helpful direct message. Someone contacts you on Telegram or X, says they are from support, and offers to walk you through the migration. They may know your handle or a detail from a public post. The goal is to get you to use their address or share your recovery words.

The lookalike domain. A site copies the design and changes one character in the address. It may even show a working wallet connection. The copy is good enough to fool a quick glance, which is why the check is the domain spelling, not the logo.

The urgency play. A message says the window is closing, the rate is about to change, or your wallet must be verified today. Urgency is used because careful people are harder to rush.

The official test

Three links decide it.

  • Website: https://olivefinance.org
  • App and migration: https://wallet.olivefinance.org
  • Support: https://t.me/olive_eng

If a link is not one of those, it is not an official migration link. That includes links sent by someone claiming to be support.

What official support never does

  • It never asks for your seed phrase or recovery words.
  • It never sends a migration address in a direct message.
  • It never asks you to send tokens to "verify" or "unlock" a wallet.
  • It never asks you to install a remote-access tool.
  • It never promises a better rate for acting fast.

If any of those happen, stop. The person is not support, whatever the profile says.

How to check before you send

  1. Close the message you received.
  2. Open the official links page from this article.
  3. Type the app address or use your own bookmark.
  4. Compare the domain character by character.
  5. Read the terms in the app, not in the message.

The extra minute is the whole defence. Most losses happen after a fast click, not after a slow check.

If you already sent something

Act in this order.

  1. Stop sending. Do not send a second "recovery" payment.
  2. Revoke any wallet approval you granted to an unknown site.
  3. Move remaining assets to a wallet you control.
  4. Collect the transaction hashes and the messages.
  5. Report the account and the domain to the platform where it happened.
  6. Ask in the official channel, https://t.me/olive_eng, for the correct next step.

No one can promise a recovery. Anyone who guarantees one and asks for a fee is running a second scam.

The recovery scam

The second scam often arrives after the first one succeeds. Someone offers to recover the lost tokens for an upfront fee, a "gas payment" or a share of the recovery.

No legitimate service can promise to reverse a transfer on a public network. A recovery offer that requires payment first is a second scam built on the first.

If you have lost funds, report the addresses and the messages. Do not pay for a promise.

Why this page exists

The CLV community has been through several migrations and rebrands. Each announcement created a fresh search spike, and each spike created impostors. The pattern is predictable, so the defence can be too.

Read the checklist before you migrate, and the CLV guide for the migration itself.

Frequently asked questions

Is t.me/olive_eng the official Telegram?

Yes. It is the only support channel we publish.

Will support ever ask for my seed phrase?

No. Never share recovery words with anyone, including someone who looks official.

Can support send me the migration address?

No. Use the address shown in the app. An address sent by message is not official.

A site looks exactly like Olive. How do I check it?

Check the domain, not the design. Only olivefinance.org and wallet.olivefinance.org are official.

I sent tokens to a fake site. Can I get them back?

Report it and ask in the official channel, but recovery is not guaranteed. Do not pay anyone who promises to recover them.
