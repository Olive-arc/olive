CLV LUX OLV OCLV: a glossary for token holders

Published 2026-11-19 · Updated 2026-11-23 · 639 words

Clover Finance is the original project, CLV is its token, Lucent Network introduced LUX, Olive is the successor, OLV is Olive's native platform token, and OCLV is Olive's representation of CLV. The initial framework phase targets CLV adoption into OCLV.

The CLV LUX OLV OCLV set is four different names with four different roles. This glossary keeps the terms in one place, with the shortest useful definition of each.

The project names

Clover Finance. The project that launched in 2021 as a cross-chain DeFi platform in the Polkadot ecosystem.

CLV. The name Clover Finance adopted in March 2022, and the name of its token.

Lucent Network. The 2025 direction for the project, built around the Solana Virtual Machine and a new token.

Olive. The successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team. Olive is a token adoption, security and interoperability framework.

The tokens

CLV. The original token. It lives on Ethereum as an ERC20 and on BNB Smart Chain as a BEP20. It is the first adopted asset in the Olive framework.

LUX. The Lucent Network token. It is a Solana SPL token introduced with the 2025 move. LUX is not part of the initial CLV phase.

OLV. Olive's native platform token. OLV is separate from adopted assets and their representations.

OCLV. Olive's representation of CLV within the Olive ecosystem. OCLV is not OLV and is not cross-chain in the initial implementation.

The actions

Swap. The 2025 move from CLV to LUX, at a 1:1 ratio, with a published window ending 17 July 2027.

Migration. The legacy term used for the move from CLV into OCLV. The app shows the current terms.

Adoption. Bringing an existing asset into the Olive framework under an approved custody, verification and representation process.

Eligibility. Who can take part. The app and current terms are the authority.

Rate. The conversion applied when a migration starts, if a rate applies. It is shown in the app.

The technical terms

ERC20. The token standard on Ethereum. CLV on Ethereum uses it.

BEP20. The token standard on BNB Smart Chain. CLV on BNB Smart Chain uses it.

SPL. The token standard on Solana. LUX uses it.

Successor. A project that continues the work under a new team. Olive is the successor to Clover and Lucent.

How the terms connect

  1. Clover Finance launches. The token is CLV.
  2. The project rebrands to CLV in 2022.
  3. CLV 2.0 becomes Lucent Network in 2025. The token becomes LUX after a 1:1 swap.
  4. Olive becomes the successor in 2026. OLV is the native platform token. CLV is adopted and represented as OCLV.

The migration guide turns the current path into practical steps.

Terms that are often confused

CLV and OCLV. CLV is the underlying asset. OCLV is Olive's representation of it.

CLV and OLV. CLV is the adopted asset. OLV is the native platform token.

OCLV and OLV. OCLV represents CLV. OLV is the native token.

LUX and OCLV. LUX is a Lucent Network asset. OCLV is Olive's representation of CLV.

Swap and migration. The swap moved CLV to LUX. The current migration target is OCLV.

Frequently asked questions

What do CLV, LUX, OLV and OCLV stand for?

CLV is the Clover token name from 2022. LUX is the Lucent Network token. OLV is Olive's native platform token. OCLV is Olive's representation of CLV.

Is OLV a new token or a renamed one?

OLV is Olive's native platform token. It is not a renamed CLV or LUX.

Is OCLV the same as OLV?

No. OCLV represents CLV. OLV is the native token.

Which one do I need for the migration?

Start with CLV. The current framework target is CLV adoption into OCLV.

Does the glossary change the terms?

No. It explains them. The app and official channels carry the current terms.
