What is Olive? The successor to Clover, explained

Published 2026-11-19 · Updated 2026-11-19 · 760 words

Olive is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team. It is built around the Olive Wallet and four connected capabilities: Connected DeFi, Quantitative Investments, Agentic Trading, and Asset and Portfolio Management. The product is in development, and it accepts CLV in the initial framework phase holders through the migration.

Olive is a new name on a long-running story. It is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team, and it is built as one platform rather than a collection of separate tools.

This page explains what Olive is, what it offers, and how former Clover and Lucent holders fit in.

What is Olive?

Olive is an intelligent finance platform built around the Olive Wallet. The wallet is the foundation. Four capabilities sit on top of it, and each one covers a stage of managing digital assets.

The product is in development. The website describes what is being built, and the roadmap sets out the phases. Nothing on the site is a promise of returns.

The four capabilities

CapabilityWhat it covers
Connected DeFiOne access layer for supported networks, protocols and opportunities
Quantitative InvestmentsResearch, models and market data for clearer decisions
Agentic TradingAuthorised agents that act within the strategy and limits you assign
Asset and Portfolio ManagementOne view of supported assets, positions and lifecycle events

The four are connected. Access finds what is reachable. Research helps you evaluate it. Execution carries out a mandate you define. Oversight shows the result.

Connected DeFi

Connected DeFi is the access pillar. It covers supported networks and assets, swaps and market access, and DeFi services such as lending, borrowing, staking and yield. The flow is connect, explore, act. Authority stays with the wallet.

Read the Connected DeFi page for the detailed product description.

Quantitative Investments

Quantitative Investments is the research pillar. It brings market intelligence, quantitative research and RWA evaluation together. The flow is gather, analyse, decide. The analysis supports the decision, and the user keeps authority.

Agentic Trading

Agentic Trading is the execution pillar. It lets you configure objectives, assets, risk and capital limits, and authorise a mandate. Agents act only within that mandate. The flow is configure, authorise, monitor, and you can review, adjust or revoke authority.

Read the agentic trading explainer for the full definition.

Asset and Portfolio Management

Asset and Portfolio Management is the oversight pillar. It brings supported assets, positions, performance and lifecycle events into one view. The flow is consolidate, allocate, monitor. No automatic rebalancing is implied.

The wallet foundation

The Olive Wallet is the starting point for every capability. It provides asset access, send and receive, swaps, transaction history, and permissions. The four pillars connect through it rather than sitting beside it.

That is the design claim. Access, research, execution and oversight share one foundation, so the user does not rebuild their setup for each task.

How Olive connects to Clover and Lucent

Olive is not a rename of Clover, and it is not Lucent under a new logo. It is a successor project, adopted by a new team, with its own platform design.

The connection is continuity for holders. The migration accepts CLV in the initial framework phase, so the people who held through the earlier stages have a path into the new platform.

The connection is not a claim about the past. It does not rewrite what happened, and it does not promise that earlier products delivered. It states who continues the work now.

Who it is for

Two groups.

Former Clover and Lucent holders. Olive is the successor, and the migration accepts CLV in the initial framework phase. All holders are eligible, with no deadline set as of 19 November 2026. Start with the CLV to OCLV migration guide or the LUX status page.

New users who want one place to work. People who find the DeFi landscape fragmented and want access, research, execution and oversight in one account.

What Olive does not claim

The product is in development. Statements about capabilities describe the design, not a delivered guarantee.

Access is not ownership. RWA products are third-party, product specific investments. Historical performance does not predict future results. Nothing on the site is financial advice.
