Clover Finance history: 2021 to the Olive era

Published 2026-11-19 · Updated 2026-11-19 · 900 words

Clover Finance launched in 2021 as a Polkadot parachain project, rebranded to CLV in March 2022, and announced CLV 2.0 and Lucent Network in January 2025. Olive is the successor, adopted by a new team, and it accepts CLV in the initial framework phase. The history explains why holders now have more than one migration path.

Clover Finance has had more than one identity. That is not unusual in crypto, but it explains why a holder from 2021 may not recognise the project in 2026.

This is a short, factual timeline. The point is not to relitigate the past. It is to show how the current migration paths came to exist.

The short version

Clover Finance launched in 2021, rebranded to CLV in 2022, and moved toward Lucent Network in 2025. Olive is the successor, adopted by a new team, and it accepts CLV in the initial framework phase.

The 2021 launch

Clover Finance arrived in 2021 with a cross-chain DeFi thesis. The project raised through a public token sale and built in the Polkadot ecosystem, where parachain slots were the competitive prize of the cycle.

CLV was the token. The pitch was access: one platform for assets and applications across chains, at a time when moving between networks was more painful than it is today.

The parachain era

Winning a parachain slot gave the project a place in Polkadot's shared security model. The work that followed focused on staking, chain infrastructure and cross-chain tooling.

That era set expectations. Holders who joined then were promised a network effect that depended on Polkadot's growth. When the market changed, the strategy had to change with it.

The quiet middle

Between the 2022 rebrand and the 2025 move, the project went through a long period of repositioning. Chain strategy shifted more than once, and communication became less frequent.

For holders, that period is the reason the later changes felt abrupt. A project that announces less is harder to read, even when the work continues.

The 2025 move

CLV 2.0 was the largest change. The project announced a move to Lucent Network, a new token, and an AI-focused direction on the Solana Virtual Machine.

The CLV to LUX swap gave holders a route at a 1:1 ratio. The published window ends 17 July 2027. That deadline belongs to the Lucent swap, not to Olive.

Why the name changed

The 2022 rebrand kept the token and the community, and shortened the public name. The documentation from that period describes CLV as Connectivity, Legible and Versatile. The token remained central to the network.

The 2025 change was larger. CLV 2.0 moved the project's direction to an AI-focused network on SVM, with a new token, LUX. That is a different technical and brand direction, not a rename.

The 2026 change is different again. Olive is a successor project, adopted by a new team. It keeps a path for existing holders rather than treating them as a closed chapter.

What this means for holders

There are two valid paths, depending on what you hold.

  • If you hold CLV, the CLV to OCLV migration is your route.
  • If you hold LUX, the LUX status is your route.

Both are open to all holders, and no deadline is set as of 19 November 2026.

If you are trying to understand the Lucent step first, read the Lucent Network status page.

What the history does not settle

A timeline explains sequence, not quality. It does not tell you whether a decision was right, whether a product delivered, or whether a token has value.

Those are separate questions. The useful takeaway is narrower. The project changed direction more than once, and each change left holders in a different position. The migration is designed around that fact.

Looking forward

Olive describes four capabilities on one wallet foundation. Connected DeFi handles access, Quantitative Investments handles research, Agentic Trading handles authorised execution, and Asset and Portfolio Management handles oversight.

You can read the summary on the What is Olive page. The product is in development, and the roadmap sets out the phases.

The reason to know the history is simple. Holders who followed every stage should not have to guess which one applies to them now. The migration answer is that both CLV are accepted.

Frequently asked questions

What was Clover Finance originally?

A 2021 Polkadot ecosystem project with a token sale, a parachain slot and a cross-chain DeFi product direction.

When did Clover become CLV?

The rebrand was announced on 10 March 2022.

What is Lucent Network?

The 2025 direction for the project, built around the Solana Virtual Machine, with a new token called LUX.

Is Olive the same team?

Olive is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team. The migration is the continuity path for holders.

Does the history affect eligibility?

No. CLV holders are eligible in the initial framework phase, with no snapshot and no deadline set.
