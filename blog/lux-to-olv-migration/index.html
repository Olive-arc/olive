LUX to OLV migration: for holders who already swapped

Published 2026-11-19 · Updated 2026-11-19 · 936 words

The LUX to OLV migration covers holders who already swapped CLV for LUX on Lucent Network. Olive accepts LUX, a Solana SPL token, and all holders are eligible with no deadline set as of 19 November 2026. The app shows the current rate and the steps, and a Solana wallet is part of the flow.

If you held CLV through 2025, you may have swapped it for LUX when Lucent Network launched. That swap was one chapter. The LUX to OLV migration is the next one.

Status checked 19 November 2026.

What changed, briefly

Clover Finance launched in 2021 and rebranded to CLV in 2022. In January 2025 the team announced CLV 2.0 and a move to Lucent Network, built around the Solana Virtual Machine. The CLV to LUX swap opened at a 1:1 ratio, with a published window ending 17 July 2027.

LUX is a Solana SPL token. Olive is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team. The LUX to OLV migration is how holders who already swapped can continue into Olive.

Who the LUX migration covers

All LUX holders are eligible. There is no deadline set as of the date at the top of this page.

This path exists because the earlier swap did not end the story for holders. A migration that ignored LUX would strand the people who followed the project furthest. Olive accepts both CLV and LUX for that reason.

If you still hold CLV instead, use the CLV to OLV guide.

What you need

  1. An Olive account. Sign in or create one at wallet.olivefinance.org.
  2. A Solana wallet you control. LUX lives on Solana, so the flow starts there.
  3. The app's current terms. The rate and the exact steps are shown in the app.
  4. A transaction record. Keep the signature or hash and the amount.

Do not send LUX to an address from a direct message. The app shows the only address that applies.

How to start

  1. Sign in to the Olive app.
  2. Open the migration page.
  3. Read the terms shown in the app, including the current rate.
  4. Follow the in-app steps for LUX from your Solana wallet.
  5. Keep your transaction record.
  6. Check that your OLV balance appears in the app after the migration completes.

If anything looks different from what the app states, stop and ask in the official channel.

The table is the fastest way to avoid a costly mistake. Lucent's swap moves you to LUX. Olive's migration moves you to OLV. They are different destinations, run by different terms.

What LUX is

LUX is a Solana SPL token. It was introduced with Lucent Network in 2025, when the project moved its technical direction to the Solana Virtual Machine.

SPL is the token standard on Solana. Holding LUX means holding a token on that network, and interacting with it means using a Solana wallet. That is the practical difference from CLV, which lives on Ethereum or BNB Smart Chain.

If you have never used a Solana wallet, set one up before you start the migration. Back up the recovery phrase offline, and test with a small amount first if the flow allows it.

Why Olive accepts LUX

A successor that accepted only CLV would ask holders who followed the project furthest to start over. Olive accepts LUX for the same reason it accepts CLV. The migration is about continuity for everyone who held through the changes.

That does not make LUX and CLV interchangeable. They are different assets on different networks, and the app treats them as separate paths. Check which one you hold before you open the migration page.

Solana wallet questions

Three questions come up often.

Which wallet? Any Solana wallet you control, provided it can sign the transaction the app asks for. The app states what it supports.

What about the recovery phrase? Back it up offline and never share it. No support person needs it, and anyone who asks is an impersonator.

What if my LUX is on an exchange? Start from a wallet you control. If the exchange does not support the migration, withdraw to your own wallet first.

The rate

The app shows the current rate. This page does not publish a number, because the terms can change and the app is the authority.

A rate you saw in a post or a screenshot is not the rate that applies to a migration you start today.

What if I never swapped to LUX

Nothing is lost. CLV is still accepted. Use the CLV to OLV guide and start from the wallet that holds your CLV.

Verify before you act

Use the official links page and read the scam warning. The only support channel we publish is t.me/olive_eng.

A migration is a moment when impersonators appear. No real support person asks for your seed phrase or recovery words. Anyone who does is not support.

Frequently asked questions

What is the LUX to OLV migration?

It is the path for Lucent Network holders to move LUX into Olive, the successor project. It accepts LUX, a Solana SPL token.

Is there a deadline for LUX holders?

No deadline is set as of 19 November 2026. Check the app and official channels for current terms.

Do I need a Solana wallet?

Yes. LUX is a Solana SPL token, so the flow starts from a Solana wallet you control.

What if I already missed the CLV to LUX swap?

You do not need it. Olive accepts CLV directly. Use the CLV guide and start from your CLV wallet.

Does the app show the rate?

Yes. The app shows the current rate and the steps. This page does not publish a number.
