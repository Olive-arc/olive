What is the CLV token? Clover's token, explained

Published 2026-11-19 · Updated 2026-11-19 · 706 words

CLV is the native token of Clover Finance, the project that rebranded to CLV in 2022. It was used for governance, network fees and staking, and it exists on Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain. In 2025 the project moved toward Lucent Network and LUX, and in 2026 Olive became the successor, accepting CLV.

CLV is one of those tokens that changed meaning as its project changed shape. It started as the Clover Finance token, became the CLV token after a rebrand, and now sits at the start of a migration into Olive.

This page explains what the CLV token is, what it was used for, and how it relates to the later tokens.

What is the CLV token?

CLV is the native token of Clover Finance, a project that launched in 2021 and rebranded to CLV in March 2022. The token was designed to serve the network, not just to sit in a wallet.

Three uses were described in the project's public material.

  • Governance. Token holders could vote on network decisions.
  • Fees. The token paid for network transactions.
  • Staking and consensus. Holders could stake CLV, directly or through a validator.

The token kept the same name through the 2022 rebrand. What changed was the brand around it.

How to check which CLV you hold

Check the network, not just the symbol. A wallet can show CLV while the token sits on Ethereum or BNB Smart Chain, and the network decides which migration path applies.

Three checks are enough. Check the standard as well as the network.

  • The network shown by the wallet.
  • The token standard, ERC20 or BEP20.
  • The balance on a block explorer.

If those disagree, stop and verify before you migrate. A block explorer is the final record for the network and the balance.

How CLV changed over time

The token's story follows the project's.

2021. Clover Finance launches, holds a public token sale, and builds in the Polkadot ecosystem. CLV is the token.

2022. The project rebrands to CLV. The token keeps its name and its role.

2025. CLV 2.0 moves the project toward Lucent Network, built around the Solana Virtual Machine. A new token, LUX, is introduced, and CLV holders are offered a 1:1 swap.

2026. Olive becomes the successor, adopted by a new team. The migration accepts CLV in the initial framework phase.

The full sequence is on the Clover Finance history page.

CLV, LUX and OCLV are not the same token

This is the most common confusion, so it is worth stating plainly.

  • CLV is the original token, on Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain.
  • LUX is the Lucent Network token, on Solana.
  • OCLV is Olive's representation of CLV. OLV is the native platform token.

They belong to different stages. Holding one does not mean you hold another. The three token comparison sets out the differences side by side.

What to do if you hold CLV

All CLV holders are eligible for the Olive migration. No deadline is set as of 19 November 2026.

Start with the CLV migration guide. It covers the path for each network, what to prepare, and how to verify the official links. If you are unsure which network you hold, use the explorer check above before you start.

This page does not publish a price or a supply figure. Those change, and a market data source is the right place to check them. The token role is the stable part.

Frequently asked questions

What is the CLV token?

The native token of Clover Finance, later the CLV project. It was used for governance, fees and staking.

Is CLV the same as Clover Finance?

Clover Finance is the project. CLV is its token, and the name the project adopted in 2022.

Does CLV still exist?

Yes. CLV on Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain is accepted by the Olive migration.

Is CLV the same as LUX or OCLV?

No. LUX is the Lucent Network token on Solana. OCLV represents CLV, and OLV is the native platform token.

Can I migrate CLV to OCLV?

Yes. All CLV holders are eligible, with no deadline set.
