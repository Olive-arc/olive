CLV adoption eligibility: who can bring CLV into Olive

Published 2026-11-19 · Updated 2026-11-23 · 262 words

The initial Olive framework phase covers CLV on Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain. LUX is not part of the initial phase. The app and current terms are the authority on eligibility for each CLV route.

Status checked 23 November 2026.

The eligibility question is simpler once the token roles are clear. CLV is the first adopted asset. OLV is the native platform token. OCLV is Olive's representation of CLV. LUX is not part of the initial phase.

CLV adoption eligibility

CLV holders on:

  • Ethereum, as an ERC20
  • BNB Smart Chain, as a BEP20

Eligibility is subject to the current terms shown in the app.

Who is not covered in the initial phase

LUX holders. LUX scope is a separate decision.

An asset with a familiar name on another network is not automatically accepted. Check the network and the contract.

What to prepare

  1. An Olive account at wallet.olivefinance.org.
  2. The wallet that holds your CLV.
  3. The correct network.
  4. A record of the amount and transaction hash.

If you hold CLV on both networks

Treat them as two routes. Keep a record for each.

If you swapped to LUX

Your LUX is not part of the initial phase. Do not send it to an olive address based on an old page or a message. Read the LUX status page.

Frequently asked questions

Are all CLV holders eligible?

The initial phase covers CLV on Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain, subject to the app terms.

Is LUX eligible?

Not in the initial phase.

Is there a snapshot?

Check the current terms in the app. This page does not publish terms that can change.

Can I migrate CLV from an exchange?

Start from a wallet you control. If your CLV is on an exchange, check whether it supports the process.
