Olive successor to Clover: what it means for holders

Published 2026-11-19 · Updated 2026-11-19 · 700 words

A successor project continues the work under a new team. Olive is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, so it accepts CLV in the initial framework phase through the migration. It is not a rename, and it does not rewrite the past. For holders, the practical effect is a path into OCLV with no deadline set.

"Successor" is a small word with a lot riding on it. It tells a holder whether the project they followed still exists, whether their token has a path, and who is responsible now.

Olive successor to Clover is a phrase worth unpacking. This page explains what it means for holders, and what it does not.

Olive successor to Clover, in plain terms

The phrase means Olive continues the work begun by Clover and Lucent, under a new team, and accepts the assets those stages produced.

What successor means

A successor is a project that continues work begun by an earlier one, under a new team. It inherits the community and the continuity question. It does not inherit every claim, product or promise made before it.

Olive is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network. It was adopted by a new team. That is the approved framing, and it shapes every page on this site.

The practical consequence is the migration. Because Olive succeeds the earlier project, it accepts the assets those stages produced. CLV has a path to OCLV.

What it is not

Four things it is not.

Not a rename. A rename keeps the same project, team and token under a new label. Olive is a different project with a new team and a new token.

Not a merger. The earlier stages were not combined into Olive. The migration is a holder path, not a corporate combination.

Not an endorsement of the past. Saying Olive succeeds Clover and Lucent is a statement about continuity. It is not a claim that every earlier decision was right, or that every earlier product delivered.

Not a guarantee. A successor continues work. It does not guarantee an outcome, a return or a timeline.

What changes for holders

Three things change.

  1. The destination. If you migrate, your asset becomes OCLV on the Olive platform.
  2. The terms. The terms that apply are the Olive terms, shown in the app, not the old project's terms.
  3. The support channel. Help comes from Olive, through the official Telegram at t.me/olive_eng.

What stays the same

Two things stay the same, and they matter.

Your eligibility is broad. CLV holders can migrate. There is no snapshot and no purchase-date rule.

You keep the choice. No deadline is set as of 19 November 2026. You can read the terms, verify the links and ask a question before you act.

Why the word matters for a decision

A holder deciding whether to migrate is really asking three questions.

  • Does the new project accept what I hold?
  • Is the path clear and reversible in the sense that I can stop before I confirm?
  • Who answers if something goes wrong?

The successor framing answers the first. The migration pages answer the second. The official channel answers the third.

If a project called itself a successor but accepted neither earlier asset, the word would be marketing. Acceptance is the test. Olive accepts CLV in the initial framework phase. The label alone proves nothing. A successor is judged by what it accepts and how it answers.

How to verify the claim yourself

Do not take this page as proof. Check three things.

  1. The assets. The migration page should show CLV as accepted.
  2. The official links. The domain should match the official links page.
  3. The terms. The app should show the rate and the steps that apply to you.

If any of those is missing, the claim is weaker than the word suggests.

Where to go next

The What is Olive page describes the platform. The Clover Finance history page covers the stages that led here. The migration guide is the path.

Frequently asked questions

Is Olive the same team as Clover?

No. Olive is a successor project, adopted by a new team.

Does successor mean Olive bought Clover?

No. It means Olive continues the work and accepts the earlier assets through the migration.

Does the successor framing affect eligibility?

No. CLV holders are eligible in the initial framework phase, with no deadline set.

Is Olive responsible for earlier promises?

The successor framing is about continuity, not about adopting every earlier claim. Read the current terms in the app.
