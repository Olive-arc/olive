How to migrate CLV to Olive: a safe checklist

Published 2026-11-19 · Updated 2026-11-19 · 575 words

To migrate CLV to Olive, sign in at wallet.olivefinance.org, open the migration page, confirm the current rate and asset, and follow the in-app steps from a wallet you control. Keep the transaction record, then check that your OLV balance appears. No deadline is set as of 19 November 2026.

A migration goes wrong for ordinary reasons. A wrong network, an address copied from the wrong place, a transaction with no record. None of it is exotic.

This checklist covers how to migrate CLV to Olive and removes the ordinary mistakes. Status checked 19 November 2026.

Before you start

  • I am on the official app at wallet.olivefinance.org.
  • I have an Olive account, or I have created one.
  • I hold CLV in a wallet I control, not on an exchange I cannot withdraw from.
  • I know whether my CLV is on Ethereum or BNB Smart Chain.
  • I have read the migration guide.
  • I have the official links page open.

The short path

  1. Check the link. Type the app address or follow it from this page. Do not use a link from a direct message or an ad.
  2. Sign in. The migration is tied to your account.
  3. Open the migration page. Read the asset, the network, the rate and the steps.
  4. Confirm the network. Ethereum for ERC20 CLV, BNB Smart Chain for BEP20 CLV.
  5. Follow the in-app steps. The app shows the address and the sequence. Use only those.
  6. Save the record. Copy the transaction hash and note the amount.
  7. Wait for completion. Follow the in-app state rather than refreshing or resending.
  8. Check your OLV balance. When the migration completes, the balance appears in the app.

If you hold LUX instead

LUX is a Solana SPL token. The path starts from a Solana wallet you control. The steps are otherwise the same. Read the LUX guide before you start.

After you migrate

Keep the transaction record until you have confirmed the balance and you no longer need it for a question. If something looks wrong, ask in the official channel with the record. Do not send a second time before you have an answer.

If a step fails

Stop at the first sign of a mismatch. Do not resend, and do not try a second address from a message.

Collect the transaction record and the exact step where it stopped. Then ask in the official channel, https://t.me/olive_eng, with those details.

A failed step is usually a network or asset mismatch. Guessing at a fix is how a small problem becomes a loss.

Do not do these

  • Do not send to an address from a direct message.
  • Do not share your seed phrase or recovery words with anyone.
  • Do not assume a rate from a screenshot still applies.
  • Do not resend while a previous attempt is unresolved.
  • Do not use a lookalike domain. Check the spelling of the domain, not just the logo.

Where to get help

The official support channel is t.me/olive_eng. Read the scam warning first, so you recognise an impersonator before one contacts you.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the CLV to OLV migration take?

The app shows the current state of your migration. Timing depends on the network and the asset, so follow the in-app status rather than a fixed estimate.

Can I migrate from an exchange?

Start from a wallet you control. If your CLV is on an exchange, check whether it supports the migration, and withdraw to your own wallet if it does not.

What if I send on the wrong network?

Use the app's stated network. If you are unsure, stop and ask in the official channel before sending. Recovery is not guaranteed.
