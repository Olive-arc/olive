Framework whitepaper status

The existing published whitepaper describes the product-suite vision. It predates the asset-agnostic framework and does not yet cover adoption, custody, representation or interoperability boundaries.

A revised framework whitepaper is in preparation. Until it is published, use the framework pages as the current summary.

What the revision will cover

  • Olive's framework identity and why it exists
  • OLV native-token role
  • External asset and representation roles
  • Adoption, custody and verification
  • Representation, accounting and reconciliation
  • Security responsibilities and residual risk
  • Existing product pillars
  • Initial and later interoperability

What remains integration-specific

Asset identity, networks, contracts, custody arrangements, ratios, fees, backing and redemption terms belong in the relevant integration document.
