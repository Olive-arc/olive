What the framework does

Olive provides a standard way for external assets to enter a controlled ecosystem. The framework handles permissions, custody, verification, representation, accounting, product access and controls.

The layers

01

Account and permissions

User authority, scoped execution, limits and revocation.

02

Adoption and custody

Asset registration, routes, custody and movement control.

03

Representation and accounting

Issuance, supply, liabilities and reconciliation.

04

Protocol access

Standard connectors and atomic execution across products.

05

Strategy and RWA lifecycle

Policy limits, valuation, restrictions and queued redemption.

06

Monitoring and controls

Simulation, anomaly detection, pauses and incident response.

07

Interoperability

Later cross-network movement with verification and lane controls.

How the flow works

  1. Permissions define what can happen.
  2. Custody secures the underlying asset.
  3. Verification confirms the transfer and evidence.
  4. Representation and accounting record the result.
  5. Strategy and connectors execute within limits.
  6. Hooks, guards and monitoring enforce the boundaries.

Built for the product stack

Olive Wallet is the shared foundation. Connected DeFi, Quantitative Investments, Agentic Trading, Asset and Portfolio Management and the RWA experience sit on top of the framework.
