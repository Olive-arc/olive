Each network has its own bridge address. Check the network and contract before sending. Do not use an address from a direct message.

What happens to CLV

Under the initial model, Olive controls the underlying CLV through its designated custody arrangement. The framework records the selected network, route and asset so the transfer can be reviewed against the current terms.

CLV is the underlying asset. OCLV is the representation. OLV is the native platform token.

Current terms

CLV is bridged to OCLV at 1 CLV to 1 OCLV. The Olive bridge is free; network fees may still apply.

OCLV is not listed on Trade. It can only be swapped to OLV through the approved OCLV → OLV swap, which carries a fee shown before confirmation.

LUX is not part of the initial CLV phase.

Verify

Use the official links and the scam warning before sending anything. Support never asks for a seed phrase or a direct-message address.
