The same project has used three token names. That is not a naming quirk. Each one belongs to a different stage, a different network and a different set of terms.

This comparison sets CLV vs LUX vs OLV side by side, so you can tell which one you hold and where it can go.

## CLV vs LUX vs OLV at a glance

| Token | Project stage | Network | Standard | Current path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLV | Clover Finance and CLV, 2021 onward | Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain | ERC20, BEP20 | Migrate to OLV |
| LUX | Lucent Network, 2025 | Solana | SPL | Migrate to OLV |
| OLV | Olive, the successor, 2026 | Olive platform | Platform token | Destination |

The table is the short answer. The rest of this page explains why the three exist.

## CLV, the original token

CLV is the native token of Clover Finance. It launched in 2021, kept its name through the 2022 rebrand to CLV, and served governance, fees and staking.

It lives on Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain. Those are the two networks the Olive migration accepts for CLV.

If you bought in 2021 or 2022 and never swapped, you likely still hold CLV. The [CLV token explainer](/blog/what-is-the-clv-token/) covers its role in more detail.

## LUX, the Lucent token

CLV 2.0 was announced in January 2025. It moved the project's direction to Lucent Network, built around the Solana Virtual Machine, and introduced a new token, LUX.

LUX is a Solana SPL token. CLV holders were offered a 1:1 swap, with a published window ending 17 July 2027.

If you swapped in 2025, you hold LUX. You do not need to reverse the swap to join Olive. The migration accepts LUX directly.

## OLV, the Olive token

Olive is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team. OLV is its token and the destination of the migration.

OLV is not a renamed CLV or LUX. It belongs to a new platform with its own product direction.

The [What is Olive](/blog/what-is-olive/) page covers that direction at product level.

## Why three tokens exist

Each token followed a change in direction.

- The 2021 token served a Polkadot-era cross-chain project.
- The 2025 token served an AI-focused network on SVM.
- The 2026 token serves the successor platform.

A project that changes direction often changes its token with it. The complication for holders is that each change left some people on the previous asset. Olive's migration exists to close that gap by accepting both earlier tokens.

The middle column is the practical part. The right column prevents the most common mistakes.

CLV holders do not need to swap to LUX before migrating. Olive accepts CLV. LUX holders do not need to reverse anything. Olive accepts LUX.

## Common confusions

**"CLV and OLV are the same, just renamed."** No. They are different stages and different tokens.

**"I have to swap to LUX first."** No. That was one path in 2025. Olive accepts CLV directly.

**"The Lucent deadline is the Olive deadline."** No. The Lucent swap has a published window ending 17 July 2027. The Olive migration has no deadline set.

**"Any token with the same symbol is the right one."** No. Check the network and the contract before you send anything.
