The same project has used several token names, and Olive's framework adds two more roles. The shortest way to avoid a costly mistake is to separate the asset from the representation and from the platform token.

This comparison sets CLV, LUX, OLV and OCLV side by side. The CLV vs LUX vs OLV vs OCLV distinction matters because each name has a different role.

## At a glance

| Token | Role | Network | Current path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLV | Original Clover token and Olive's first adopted asset | Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain | Adopt into Olive as OCLV |
| LUX | Lucent Network token | Solana | Not part of the initial CLV phase |
| OLV | Olive's native platform token | Olive platform | Separate from adopted assets |
| OCLV | Olive's representation of CLV | Olive ecosystem | Created through the approved CLV adoption process |

## CLV, the adopted asset

CLV is the original Clover token. It lives on Ethereum as an ERC20 and on BNB Smart Chain as a BEP20.

Under Olive's framework, CLV is the first adopted underlying asset. Olive controls the underlying CLV through its designated custody arrangement.

If you hold CLV, the [CLV to OCLV guide](/blog/clv-to-olv-migration/) is the place to start.

## LUX, the Lucent token

CLV 2.0 was announced in January 2025. It moved the project's direction to Lucent Network and introduced LUX, a Solana SPL token.

LUX is not part of the initial Olive CLV phase. The current framework target is CLV adoption into OCLV. LUX is a separate scope question.

## OLV, the native platform token

OLV is Olive's native platform token.

OLV is not the representation of CLV. It is not a wrapped or renamed CLV. Its supply, utility, staking, governance and allocation are governed by a separate OLV specification.

## OCLV, the representation of CLV

OCLV is Olive's representation of CLV within the Olive ecosystem.

It is not OLV. It is not a cross-chain token in the initial implementation.

The issuance, transfer and redemption rules for OCLV are part of the framework's approved terms. The app shows the terms that apply to a migration you start.

## Common confusions

**"CLV and OLV are the same."** No. CLV is the adopted asset. OLV is Olive's native platform token.

**"OCLV and OLV are the same."** No. OCLV represents CLV. OLV is the native token.

**"I have to swap to LUX first."** No. LUX is not part of the initial CLV phase. Start with CLV.

**"The Lucent deadline is the Olive deadline."** No. Lucent's swap has a published window ending 17 July 2027. Olive's terms are shown in the app.

**"Any token with the same symbol is the right one."** No. Check the network and the contract before you send anything.
