The Lucent mainnet was the moment the 2025 direction stopped being an announcement and became infrastructure. For CLV holders, it also fixed the destination of the swap: LUX on Solana.

This page covers what launched, in plain terms, and what it meant for holders.

## The short timeline

| Date | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 14 January 2025 | CLV 2.0 announced, with a move to Lucent Network and SVM |
| February 2025 | The 1:1 CLV to LUX swap is described |
| March 2025 | Lucent test phase launched |
| July 2025 | Lucent mainnet announced |
| August 2025 | Lucent Live AMA recaps the launch and the swap |

The dates come from the project's own documentation and announcements, linked under Sources.

## Lucent Network mainnet: what launched

The Lucent Network mainnet was the public arrival of the 2025 direction. It is worth separating the announcement from the infrastructure it described. The announcement is the claim. The infrastructure is the real test.

## What launched

The July announcement said the mainnet was live, with an RPC endpoint, a bridge and an explorer. The network was described as built on the Solana Virtual Machine, powered by the SOON stack.

In practical terms, three things arrived at once.

- **A network to use.** The SVM environment became available beyond a trial phase.
- **Tools to reach it.** The RPC, bridge and explorer are the basic access layer for any network.
- **A token to use on it.** LUX, as a Solana SPL token, is the asset the network was built around.

Whether those pieces performed as described is a separate question. This page reports what was announced, not an independent audit.

## Why the SVM direction mattered

The 2025 plan was not a small upgrade. It moved the project from its earlier chain strategy to an environment built for high-throughput applications and AI agents.

That is why the branding changed too. Lucent was presented as an AI-focused network, not as a continuation of the earlier cross-chain product.

For holders, the technical detail mattered less than the token consequence. The new network came with a new token, and the old token needed a path to it.

## What it meant for CLV holders

The CLV to LUX swap was the holder-facing part of the move. It offered a 1:1 conversion from CLV on Ethereum or BNB Smart Chain to LUX on Solana.

The swap has a published window ending 17 July 2027. It is a separate migration from the later Olive migration, and the two should not be confused.

If you swapped, you hold LUX. If you did not, you still hold CLV. Both have a path forward now.

## What came after the mainnet

The August 2025 AMA described the mainnet as live and the swap portal as open, with manual and semi-automatic flows for exchanges.

After that, the next major change for holders was Olive. Olive is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team, and it accepts CLV and LUX.

That means the mainnet was not the final destination for holders. It was a stage. The next stage, Olive, accepts LUX and CLV.

## How to read this history

Two cautions.

**Announcements are not audits.** A mainnet announcement describes what the team says launched. It does not prove performance, security or adoption.

**A stage is not a verdict.** The Lucent stage can be understood without deciding whether it succeeded. A launch date is a start, not a scorecard. The useful question for a holder is narrower: what do I hold now, and where can it go?

## Frequently asked questions

### When did the Lucent mainnet launch?

The project announced it in July 2025.

### What technology did it use?

The Solana Virtual Machine, with the SOON stack, and LUX as a Solana SPL token.

### Did the mainnet change my CLV?

No. CLV stayed CLV. The swap was a separate, optional step that moved CLV to LUX.

### Can I still migrate LUX to Olive?

Yes. Olive accepts LUX, with no deadline set as of 19 November 2026.
