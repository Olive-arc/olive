Clover Finance holders have more than one asset and more than one migration behind them. That is why a single answer is hard to find. This page is the map.

Status checked 19 November 2026.

## The short version

Olive is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team. It accepts CLV and LUX. All holders are eligible. No deadline is set. The app shows the current rate and the steps.

If you want the detailed path for your asset, use one of these.

## Pick your path

| You hold | Network | Standard | Path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLV | Ethereum | ERC20 | CLV to OLV migration |
| CLV | BNB Smart Chain | BEP20 | CLV to OLV migration |
| LUX | Solana | SPL | LUX to OLV migration |

If your asset is not in the table, check the app before you act. A token with a familiar name on another chain is not automatically the asset the migration accepts.

## The two migrations people confuse

There are two migrations in this story, and they go to different places.

| Migration | From | To | Window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lucent swap | CLV | LUX | Published end 17 July 2027 |
| Olive migration | CLV or LUX | OLV | No deadline set |

Lucent's swap moved CLV to LUX in 2025. Olive's migration moves CLV or LUX to OLV. The first ends on a published date. The second has no deadline set.

If you hold LUX, you do not need to reverse anything. Olive accepts LUX directly.

## Eligibility in one paragraph

All CLV and LUX holders are eligible. There is no snapshot, no minimum, no maximum, and no purchase-date rule. Holders from 2021, holders who bought later, holders who swapped to LUX, and holders who hold both assets are all covered.

The full detail is on the [eligibility page](/blog/clv-migration-eligibility/).

## What to have ready

1. An Olive account at [wallet.olivefinance.org](https://wallet.olivefinance.org).
2. The wallet that holds your CLV or LUX, and the ability to sign from it.
3. The right network for your asset.
4. A record of the amount and the transaction hash or signature.
5. The [official links page](/blog/clv-migration-official-links/) open.

## How the migration runs, at a high level

The public flow is simple.

1. Sign in to the app.
2. Open the migration page.
3. Read the asset, the network, the current rate and the steps.
4. Follow the in-app steps from the wallet that holds the asset.
5. Keep the record.
6. Check that your OLV balance appears in the app.

The app carries the live terms. This page does not publish a rate, because a fixed number would go stale and the app is the authority.

## If you hold both CLV and LUX

Treat them as two migrations. Keep a separate record for each, and start each one from the wallet that holds the asset, on the network where it sits.

You do not need to consolidate first. If one path is unclear, ask before you start the other, so a question about one does not block the other.

## Why the rate is in the app

A public page cannot hold a live rate without going stale. A stale rate is worse than no rate, because it invites a mismatch between what you read and what the app applies.

The app shows the term that applies when you start the migration. Read it before you confirm. Do not plan around a number from a screenshot or a post.

## What to do if something looks wrong

Stop. Do not resend, and do not try a second address from a message.

Collect the record and the exact step where it stopped, then ask in the official channel. A mismatch is usually a network or asset issue, and guessing at a fix is how a small problem becomes a loss.

## What the migration is not

It is not a race. No deadline is set, so a holder can verify the project, read the terms and ask a question before acting.

It is not a way to recover tokens lost in an earlier bridge or swap. It starts from the asset you hold today.

It is not a promise about the earlier project. Olive is a successor project, and the migration is the continuity path for holders.

It is not a guarantee of returns. Nothing here is financial advice, and digital assets carry risk.

It is not a reason to trust a message. Migration searches attract impersonators. Read the [scam warning](/blog/clv-migration-scams/) before you send anything.
