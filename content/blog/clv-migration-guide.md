Clover Finance holders have more than one asset and more than one migration behind them. That is why a single answer is hard to find. This page is the current map.

## The short version

Olive is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team. The framework target is:

- OLV, Olive's native platform token
- CLV, the first adopted underlying asset
- OCLV, Olive's representation of CLV

This CLV migration guide covers the current path. The migration target is OCLV. It is not OLV.

## Pick your path

| You hold | Network | Standard | Current path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLV | Ethereum | ERC20 | Adopt into Olive as OCLV |
| CLV | BNB Smart Chain | BEP20 | Adopt into Olive as OCLV |
| LUX | Solana | SPL | Not part of the initial phase |

If your asset is not in the table, check the app before you act.

## The two events people confuse

| Event | From | To | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lucent swap | CLV | LUX | Published window ending 17 July 2027 |
| Olive migration | CLV | OCLV | Current framework phase |

Lucent's swap moved CLV to LUX in 2025. Olive's current migration targets OCLV. They are different events.

## Eligibility in one paragraph

CLV holders on Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain are the initial framework case, subject to the app terms. LUX is not part of the initial phase.

The full detail is on the [eligibility page](/blog/clv-migration-eligibility/).

## What to have ready

1. An Olive account at [wallet.olivefinance.org](https://wallet.olivefinance.org).
2. The wallet that holds your CLV, and the ability to sign from it.
3. The right network for your CLV.
4. A record of the amount and the transaction hash.
5. The [official links page](/blog/clv-migration-official-links/) open.

## How the migration runs, at a high level

1. Sign in to the app.
2. Open the migration page.
3. Read the asset, network, current rate and steps.
4. Follow the in-app steps from the wallet that holds CLV.
5. Keep the record.
6. Check the balance shown in the app.

Status checked 23 November 2026.

The app carries the live terms.

## Why the rate is in the app

A public page cannot hold a live rate without going stale. The app shows the term that applies when you start the migration.

## What the migration is not

It is not a race. Check the current terms before acting.

It is not a way to recover tokens lost in an earlier bridge or swap. It starts from the asset you hold today.

It is not a promise about the earlier project.

It is not a guarantee of returns. Nothing here is financial advice.

It is not a reason to trust a message. Read the [scam warning](/blog/clv-migration-scams/) before you send anything.
