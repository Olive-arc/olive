Status checked 23 November 2026.

A CLV migration checklist is easier to review when the steps are boring. This checklist keeps the sequence short.

## Before you start

1. **Check the link.** Type the app address or follow it from the official links page. Do not use a link from a direct message or an ad.
2. **Confirm the asset.** CLV is the initial framework asset.
3. **Confirm the network.** Ethereum or BNB Smart Chain.
4. **Read the terms in the app.** The current rate and steps are shown there.

## CLV migration checklist: during the process

5. **Use a wallet you control.** Do not start from an address you cannot sign from.
6. **Keep the record.** Copy the transaction hash and note the amount.
7. **Follow the in-app state.** Do not resend because a page looks slow.

## After you submit

8. **Review the balance shown in the app.**
9. **Keep the record until the request is resolved.**
10. **Ask in the official channel if something does not match.** Do not try a second address from a message.

## What the destination is

The destination representation is **OCLV**, Olive's representation of CLV.

OLV is Olive's native platform token. It is not the migration destination.

LUX is not part of the initial phase.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take?

Follow the status shown in the app. This page does not publish a fixed time.

### What if I sent the wrong network?

Stop and ask in the official channel with the transaction record. Do not send a second transfer to an address from a message.

### What balance should appear?

The app shows the current terms and the resulting balance. This page does not publish a rate or amount.
