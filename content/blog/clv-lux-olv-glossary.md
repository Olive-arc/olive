CLV LUX OLV is a short way to name three stages of one project. Migration pages use a lot of names, so this glossary keeps the CLV, LUX and OLV terms in one place, with the shortest useful definition of each.

## The project names

**Clover Finance.** The project that launched in 2021 as a cross-chain DeFi platform in the Polkadot ecosystem.

**CLV.** The name Clover Finance adopted in March 2022, and the name of its token.

**Lucent Network.** The 2025 direction for the project, built around the Solana Virtual Machine and a new token.

**Olive.** The successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team.

## The tokens

**CLV.** The original token. It lives on Ethereum as an ERC20 and on BNB Smart Chain as a BEP20. It was used for governance, fees and staking.

**LUX.** The Lucent Network token. It is a Solana SPL token, introduced with the 2025 move.

**OLV.** The Olive token. It is the destination of the current migration.

## The actions

**Swap.** The 2025 move from CLV to LUX, at a 1:1 ratio, with a published window ending 17 July 2027.

**Migration.** The move from CLV or LUX into OLV. It has no deadline set as of 19 November 2026.

**Eligibility.** Who can migrate. All CLV and LUX holders are eligible.

**Rate.** The conversion applied when you start a migration. It is shown in the app.

## The technical terms

**ERC20.** The token standard on Ethereum. CLV on Ethereum uses it.

**BEP20.** The token standard on BNB Smart Chain. CLV on BNB Smart Chain uses it.

**SPL.** The token standard on Solana. LUX uses it.

**Successor.** A project that continues the work under a new team. Olive is the successor to Clover and Lucent.

## How the terms connect

The sequence is the simplest way to remember them.

1. Clover Finance launches. The token is CLV.
2. The project rebrands to CLV in 2022.
3. CLV 2.0 becomes Lucent Network in 2025. The token becomes LUX after a 1:1 swap.
4. Olive becomes the successor in 2026. The token is OLV, and the migration accepts CLV or LUX.

That is four names on one timeline. The [migration guide](/blog/clv-migration-guide/) turns the timeline into a path.

## Terms that are often confused

**CLV and OLV.** Different tokens and different stages. OLV is not a renamed CLV.

**LUX and OLV.** LUX belongs to Lucent. OLV belongs to Olive.

**Swap and migration.** The swap moved CLV to LUX. The migration moves CLV or LUX to OLV.

**Deadline and window.** Lucent's swap has a published window. The Olive migration has no deadline set.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do CLV, LUX and OLV stand for?

CLV is the Clover token name from 2022. LUX is the Lucent Network token. OLV is the Olive token.

### Is OLV a new token or a renamed one?

A new token for the successor project, not a rename of CLV or LUX.

### Which one do I need for the migration?

Either CLV or LUX. Both are accepted. OLV is the destination.

### Does the glossary change the terms?

No. It explains them. The app and the official channels carry the current terms.
