## What each one is for

**The website** carries the product description, the roadmap and the blog. It is not the place to start a migration.

**The app** is where the migration happens. Sign in, open the migration page, and read the terms shown there.

**Telegram** is the published support channel. Use it to ask a question, not to receive an address. Any address sent to you in a direct message is not official.

**X, Medium and Paragraph** carry announcements. An announcement can tell you that something changed. It cannot replace the app's terms.

**The whitepaper** is the long-form document. Read it for context, not for a live rate.

## How to verify a link

1. Check the domain spelling character by character. Lookalikes change one letter or add a word.
2. Type the address or use a bookmark. Do not click a link from a direct message or an ad.
3. Check that the page is served over HTTPS.
4. Compare the page against this list before you connect a wallet.
5. If a page asks for a seed phrase, close it. No official page needs one.

## Bookmark the app

The safest link is the one you saved yourself. Bookmark the app after you confirm the domain, and use the bookmark for every later visit.

A bookmark does not protect you from a fake page you saved by mistake. Check the domain once more before you sign in, and compare it against this list.

## What is not official

- Direct messages offering help with a migration.
- Ads that promise a better rate or a fast track.
- Accounts that ask you to send tokens to "verify" a wallet.
- Domains that copy the design but not the address.
- Anyone who asks for your recovery words.
