Eligibility questions are usually where a migration loses people. A holder reads that a snapshot passed, or that a wallet must have held before a date, and assumes they are too late.

For the Olive migration, the public terms are simpler. This page states who is eligible and what to prepare.

Status checked 19 November 2026.

## CLV migration eligibility

All CLV holders are eligible. All LUX holders are eligible. There is no snapshot date, no minimum holding, and no maximum.

That covers people who bought at launch in 2021, people who bought later, people who held through the Lucent swap, and people who hold both assets today.

The migration is open to all holders because the point is continuity. Olive is the successor to Clover Finance and Lucent Network, adopted by a new team.

## Which assets are accepted

| Asset | Network | Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CLV | Ethereum | ERC20 |
| CLV | BNB Smart Chain | BEP20 |
| LUX | Solana | SPL |

If your asset sits on a different network, check the app before you act. A token with the same name on another chain is not automatically the asset the migration accepts.

## What "all holders" means in practice

Three cases come up most often.

**You held since 2021.** You are eligible. The 2021 token sale does not create a separate class, and there is no early-holder-only window.

**You already swapped to LUX.** You are eligible. Use the [LUX guide](/blog/lux-to-olv-migration/).

**You hold both CLV and LUX.** You can migrate both. Treat them as separate assets and keep a record for each.

## If your tokens sit on an exchange

The migration starts from a wallet you control. If your CLV or LUX is on an exchange, check whether that exchange supports the migration. If it does not, withdraw to your own wallet before you start.

This is the step people most often skip. An exchange is not a wallet you control, and a withdrawal address is not the same as a wallet that can sign.

## What to prepare

1. An Olive account at [wallet.olivefinance.org](https://wallet.olivefinance.org).
2. The wallet that holds your CLV or LUX, and the ability to sign from it.
3. The right network for the asset.
4. A record of what you send, including the hash or signature and the amount.
5. The official links page open, so you can check every address against it.

## What changed at each stage

Eligibility is easier to trust when you know why it is broad.

The 2021 holders bought the original token. The 2022 rebrand kept the token and the community. The 2025 move introduced LUX and asked holders to swap. Each stage created a group of holders with a different asset.

A rule that recognised only one group would exclude people who did exactly what the project asked at the time. The public terms avoid that by accepting CLV and LUX and treating all holders as eligible.

## Records worth keeping

Keep four records for each migration you start.

- The asset and the network you sent from.
- The amount.
- The transaction hash or signature.
- The date and the app page you used.

These records answer most questions faster than a screenshot. They also help if you need to ask support for help.


## What is not published

The rate and the exact steps are shown in the app. They are not fixed on this page, because terms can change and the app is the authority.

No deadline is set as of the date above. That is a statement about today, not a guarantee about every future date. This page is updated when those terms change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a snapshot for eligibility?

No. The public terms say all CLV and LUX holders are eligible, with no snapshot cut-off.

### Is there a minimum or maximum amount?

No public minimum or maximum is stated. The app shows the terms that apply to your migration.

### I bought CLV after 2022. Am I eligible?

Yes. There is no purchase-date rule in the public terms.

### Can I migrate both CLV and LUX?

Yes. They are separate assets and can both be migrated.
